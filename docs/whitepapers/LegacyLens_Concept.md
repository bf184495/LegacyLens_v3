# LegacyLens

The Digital Legacy Operating System

## Problem

Modern digital lives are fragmented.

Photos live in one location.

Email exists elsewhere.

Documents and calendars often remain disconnected.

Historical context is frequently lost.

## Solution

LegacyLens creates a unified archive platform.

The platform ingests:

- Photos
- Contacts
- Documents
- Email
- Calendar Data

and constructs a relationship graph capable of preserving both content and context.

## Future Vision

The Legacy Librarian AI acts as a:

- Archivist
- Historian
- Preservation Specialist
- Research Assistant

for personal digital archives.
