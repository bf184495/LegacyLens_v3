# Archive Intelligence Vision

LegacyLens V3 is evolving beyond a digital archive management system.

The long-term objective is to create an Archive Intelligence Platform capable of understanding relationships between digital assets and reconstructing historical context.

## Core Philosophy

Files are not the goal.

Understanding is the goal.

LegacyLens should help users:

- Understand their archives
- Improve archive quality
- Recover missing context
- Preserve important information
- Transfer digital legacy to future generations

## Archive Intelligence

Future Archive Intelligence will enable:

- Metadata restoration
- Event reconstruction
- Timeline generation
- Relationship discovery
- Historical significance analysis
- Preservation recommendations

## Cross Module Intelligence

Future analysis will connect:

- Photos
- Contacts
- Documents
- Email
- Calendar
- Knowledge

into a unified understanding of people, places, events, and relationships.

## Long-Term Goal

Create a Digital Legacy Operating System capable of understanding and preserving the story behind a person's digital life.
