LegacyLens Consumer Interest Survey
Introduction

Thank you for taking this brief survey.

LegacyLens is a new software platform being designed to help individuals preserve, organize, understand, and eventually transfer their digital archives, including photos, documents, emails, contacts, and historical records.

This survey is intended to measure consumer interest, concerns, and potential value.
